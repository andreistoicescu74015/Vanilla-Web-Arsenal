Contrast checker

Check text and background colors against WCAG 2.2. Any CSS color works: hex, rgb(), hsl(), oklch() or a name like navy.

Colors

Large text means at least 18pt (about 24px), or 14pt bold (about 18.5px bold). The ratio is never rounded up: 4.499:1 fails 4.5:1.
