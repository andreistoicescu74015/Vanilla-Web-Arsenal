Is Nimbus really free for students?

Yes. The Student plan has no time limit. You only pay if you need more than 5 members or 3 boards.

Can our mentor see the board without an account?

Yes. Share a read-only link from the board menu. You can turn it off at any time.

Does it work on phones?

Nimbus works in any modern mobile browser, and you can add it to your home screen.

What happens to our data after the hackathon?

It stays until you delete it. Export everything as CSV whenever you want.
